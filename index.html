About

Hi, I'm Hunter Vermeer

I started HV Photography because I believed real estate agents deserved better than the generic, flat photos that were flooding the MLS. Every listing tells a story — and I became obsessed with telling it well.

Based in Dallas, I work directly with agents and homeowners across the DFW area. When you book with me, you're not handing off to a team — you get me on-site, behind the lens, every single shoot. That personal attention shows in the final product.

I take pride in delivering images that make buyers stop, look twice, and want to schedule a showing. Your listing gets 24-hour turnaround, zero hassle, and photos you'll be proud to put in front of clients.
